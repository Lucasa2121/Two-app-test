How to get your KPI PDF

  1. Go to apps.cellularworld.net and log in.
  2. Open Opus X and log in.
  3. From the dropdown menu, select Reports > Sales > Sales Performance Report.
  4. Under Report Type, choose By Employee.
  5. Under Employees, select Unselect All, then choose your name.
  6. Select View Report and wait for it to load.
  7. Click the 💾 Save icon, then choose PDF.
  8. On iPhone: tap Open > Share > Save to Files.
  9. Return to this app and tap Upload to select the saved PDF.

Overall Progress

Green — Met: All seven KPIs meet their current month-to-date targets.

Yellow — Almost There: No KPI is red, but at least one is within 75% of target or within one base unit.

Red — Not Met: At least one KPI is below the Almost There threshold.

Based only on the latest confirmed calculation; shift history and Employee GP do not affect this status. The number inside the active light is the equal-weight average of each KPI’s progress toward its current MTD target, capped at 100% per KPI. The light color continues to use the existing official status rules.

How Daily Shift Priority works

Uses your latest confirmed month-to-date KPI actuals and manually entered shifts worked. Each KPI is compared with its cumulative target.

When behind, the next-shift recovery requirement is the MTD shortfall plus the KPI's base target (one whole unit for grouped KPIs). KPIs already at or above target show zero recovery needed.

Red KPIs appear first, then yellow, then green. Within a color, larger requirements come first. History notes are informational only and never change ranking, official calculations, or status.

How Shift Progress Tracker works

Upload one cumulative KPI PDF after each worked shift, enter that shift number, and press Calculate. For consecutive shift snapshots, the app subtracts the previous MTD total from the latest to find sales during that shift, then subtracts the normal per-shift KPI target.

The horizontal axis is shift number; the vertical axis is sales above (+) or below (−) target. Zero means exactly on target. If a shift is missing, the app does not invent its results. Shift 1 can be calculated directly when its first report contains only that shift's sales.

Select a KPI below the chart to show only its line; Clear selection shows all seven. History resets each calendar month and never affects AGT/ADT or overall progress.

How What If works

Change the values in the What if…? column to explore possible adjusted daily targets. The What If table updates immediately using the same formulas as the official calculator.

These changes do not affect your official results, Daily Shift Priority, traffic light, Employee GP, or saved shift history. Press Calculate to commit pending upload values, shift count, and PDF-extracted Employee GP—not What If values.

About Fast Start

Fast Start measures your month-to-date PDF results against separate monthly goals during the first seven calendar days of each month.

Yellow: at least 33% of a goal. Green: at least 50%. Red: below 33%. Each progress bar is scaled so 50% of the monthly goal fills the bar (100% Fast Start progress). The gray marker at 66% of the bar represents the 33% minimum; the gray end marker represents the 50% preferred goal.

Dollar-based GP and APO use dollar amounts. Sales KPIs use whole-number counts. The percentage is actual divided by monthly goal; reaching 33% or 50% is evaluated using the unrounded percentage.

Progress persists on this device and starts a new period on the first of each month. A report covering the 7th or earlier can lock in your deadline result. If no qualifying report was saved, later MTD values are shown as provisional and cannot prove the day-7 result.

Volume-based metrics only: This Fast Start section tracks only volume-based performance metrics. For percentage-based Fast Start metrics, including Protection and Premium Mix, refer to the board.

Fast Start goals are separate from official AGT/ADT targets. No official calculation is affected.
